Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

HandMadeProjects does not require visitor accounts, logins, or registration to browse the site. We use privacy-respecting analytics (Ahrefs Web Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic patterns such as page views and referral sources. This analytics script may set cookies in your browser.

How we use it

Aggregate analytics data helps us understand which pages and projects are most useful to visitors, so we can improve the catalog and site experience. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Contact information you share with us

If you reach out via Call or WhatsApp using the contact details on this site, any information you choose to share (your name, project requirements, etc.) is used solely to respond to your query and is not shared with third parties.

Cookies

We use a small "cookie consent" preference stored in your browser's local storage to remember that you've acknowledged this policy, plus your dark/light theme preference. Neither is shared with us or any third party — they stay in your browser.
